# Testing positional information and query mediation in AI-08

This follow-up is authorized after the completed fixed-signal study and its stopping decision. It tests an explanation of the observed order-dependent benefit and harm. It does not retroactively change that study's threshold, reopen its confirmation set for fitting, or count a new experiment as a preregistered success of the old protocol.

## Hypothesis and scope

The correction may predominantly encode which earlier name position should be avoided. With source and recipient order aligned, downstream copying favors the intended name. With opposite order, the same positional instruction can favor the wrong name. An identity-specific semantic correction is an alternative; downstream changes in copied content, keys, nonlinear processing and distributed pathways are also alternatives.

Known GPT-2 Medium mover heads provide a bounded candidate set: zero-indexed **14.14, 15.14, 16.15, 17.4 and 19.15**. These are explicitly listed in Appendix C.3 of [Circuit Component Reuse Across Tasks in Transformer Language Models](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/4ffbfbcf4ad7304d57158b046525e46c-Paper-Conference.pdf). The study extends the token/position distinction in [Interpretability in the Wild](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.00593) to this particular fixed-source assay. It would be a targeted causal explanation/replication, not discovery of a new universal routing principle. No whole-model head search is planned. The original paper has a 13.13/13.14 inconsistency for an inhibition head; those ambiguous inhibition-head lists are not used to select our targets.

## Inputs and conditions

Development uses the old AI-08 development recipients and pairs their name units with old pilot-holdout names as foreign source identities. It is for adapter validation and descriptive feasibility, not independent evidence. Confirmation has eight entirely new name units and eight new location/object contexts. Each name unit contains one recipient triple and one disjoint foreign-source triple, so donor dependence stays within the resampled unit. Each bank crosses two roles and four wording/order cells: 128 development recipients and 512 confirmation recipients. Names are selected by deterministic tokenization-only rules, with no outcome-based replacement. All 48 confirmation names are disjoint from the prior AI-08 names; no separation from pretraining is claimed.

Retain the same twelve source heads, additive repeated-subject-position intervention, source context and model checkpoint as before. Each source clean/corrupt pair is measured once and cached. For the primary identity/order factorial, scale each projected head correction to the corresponding same-identity canonical-source head norm. The reference norm is fixed per name-role unit, not chosen per recipient order or outcome. This produces four cached source vectors per unit/role: same/foreign identity crossed with source order 0/1. Each recipient labels these as matching/opposite order. Preserve the raw-source conditions as secondary controls for effects introduced by norm adjustment.

Each recipient has 45 condition records: zero; four normalized source conditions; four raw source conditions; recipient-self correction; an independent zero write; and for each normalized source, five candidate-set clamps (Q, K, V, P, Z), two layer-matched random-set Q clamps and a query-only transplant. Two additional query transplants rotate the same-identity source query differences through fixed orthogonal 64-dimensional rotations, preserving each head's query-change norm and within-head distances while changing direction. Random head sets and rotations are fixed before outcomes.

Q clamps the selected heads' final-position query to that recipient's unedited corrupted query. K clamps their keys only on the path to the final-position output; V analogously clamps copied content. P clamps the complete final-position attention probability vector. Z clamps the final-position head output. Other query positions and unselected heads are untouched by the explicit intervention, but later computations respond normally. These are path-specific interventions, not replacement of all global key/value uses. Query-only transplants start with no source correction and replace only these final-position queries with those measured under the relevant source correction in that same recipient. All downstream computations are recomputed; this is a bounded interventional mediation assay rather than a uniquely identified natural indirect effect.

Save full vocabulary logits, residuals, final-position Q/P/Z observations across all heads in the five candidate layers, the baseline K/V cache, source hashes and condition metadata. Measure full clean competence; retain every example regardless of competence. Use a constant batch shape of eight identical recipient prompts, padding unused slots and counting their inference cost. This avoids the earlier singleton/batch discrepancy; no old tolerance is retroactively changed.

## Numerical qualification

Before interpreting any new mechanism result, compare the wrapped unedited model with the original adapter at the same batch shape; test explicit zero writes, exact repeat and no-effect Q/K/V/P/Z clamps at their own baseline. Compare joint P-and-V clamping with independent Z clamping. Require maximum absolute logit/state error <=0.0005 and zero full-vocabulary top-1 changes. Validate rotation orthogonality and query-difference norms. If a test fails, preserve the run, repair or investigate the adapter and qualify again before continuation. Do not relax the tolerance to make a check pass.

Run confirmation once after qualification and the entire runner/analysis are frozen. The fixed literature head set, factorial and analysis do not change after development outcomes; no head-set optimization on this or the prior confirmation bank is permitted. If clean competence fails the prior 85% cell-level minimum or the source contrasts vanish, report that limitation and do not assert a mechanism for an inadequate assay.

## Primary causal quantities

Let D be mean intended-answer margin under matched minus opposite normalized source correction, pooled over roles/cells. Compute this separately for same and foreign identities. A positive foreign contrast would show that sharing the particular names is not necessary for the positional effect. The original behavior was measured at source strength +1; this follow-up fixes that strength and cannot establish local linearity or dose generality.

For same identities define query necessity as `(D − D_Qclamp) / D`; query sufficiency as `D_query_only / D`; foreign retention as `D_foreign / D`. Ratios use aggregate means, never averages of unstable individual ratios. If D<=0.25 logit units, these ratios are treated as uninterpretable and the corresponding mechanistic claims fail. Also report absolute effects, sign-specific repair/damage, the two random head controls, rotation control, K/V/P/Z clamps, all foreign mediation conditions, answer accuracy, entropy, KL from each recipient baseline, and attention mass on IO, S1 and S2. No baseline-correctness filtering enters the primary estimate.

Inference independently resamples the eight paired name units and eight lexical contexts, retaining all within-unit donors, roles, wording cells and conditions. Use 20,000 draws with seed 2026100814. The three primary ratios receive **98.3333% percentile intervals** (Bonferroni family of three); 95% intervals on other contrasts are descriptive. Invalid bootstrap denominators are counted rather than silently discarded. If more than 1% of resamples have D<=0.25, do not make the joint mechanistic claim.

Support the bounded explanation only if clean competence and numerical qualification pass, same and foreign positional contrasts are positive, all three primary ratios have point estimates >=0.5 and simultaneous lower bounds above zero, and the same-identity order contrast is positive in at least six of eight name units and six of eight contexts. For a query-specific interpretation, additionally require query necessity to exceed the mean random-head necessity and V-clamp necessity by at least 0.25 of D, and query sufficiency to exceed the rotation control by at least 0.25 of D; report descriptive 95% intervals for these specificity checks and require their lower bounds above zero. A passing result identifies a sufficient and necessary-in-part query route for this intervention effect; it does not prove that all context dependence is routing or that these five heads form the complete circuit.

If the criteria fail, finish the controls, audit and scoped explanation of what was learned. Do not tune thresholds, enlarge the head set or repeatedly add names until the result passes. Any materially different mechanism would need a separately declared design.
